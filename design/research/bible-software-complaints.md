# Complaints users report about popular Bible study software

Researched 2026-11-16 (web search and page fetches). Apps covered: Logos, Olive Tree, Blue Letter Bible (BLB), YouVersion, Bible Gateway, e-Sword, Accordance, theWord.

## Method and honest limits

- Many primary sources blocked fetching (the Logos and Accordance forums returned 403; the App Store review page was 404). For those, the claim rests on the search tool's excerpt of the page, not a full read of it. Those entries are marked "(excerpt)".
- No Reddit threads were retrievable directly. One Reddit-aggregator page was read (gummysearch), and it holds few complaints.
- Several sources are low-grade: a review-roundup blog with no stated method (unstar.app), AI-generated summary pages (marlvel.ai), and Trustpilot, which is self-selected. Prevalence below is a rough guide, not a count. "Widespread" means it appears in several unrelated sources. "Recurring" means two or three. "Scattered" means one or two.
- Nothing here is a verdict on any app. Pages were paraphrased and no scripture was quoted.

## Themes

### 1. Cost, subscriptions and ownership (widespread)

- Logos: the move to subscription is the most-repeated complaint. A long-time buyer with a large library objects to a recurring fee for books already owned. The article's author replies that earlier purchases keep working and that a lapse only stops new features. https://www.knowableword.com/2025/11/21/logos-bible-software-the-subscription-model-seems-to-be-working/
- Logos on Trustpilot: automatic renewals with refusals to refund, an annual plan renewing the same day as the notice, and doubt over whether paid books stay available after a subscription ends. A claim that a lifetime membership was treated as invalid also appears. Small, self-selected sample. https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/logos.com
- Logos packaging: a roundup lists overlapping base packages, inconsistent credit when upgrading tiers, and unclear subscription-versus-owned status (weak source). https://unstar.app/blog/youversion-bible-gateway-olive-tree-logos-blue-letter-bible-apps-ranked-2026
- Full-power cost: one comparison puts Logos at roughly $500 to $2,000+ and calls the Faithlife pricing complex. https://www.teachfloor.com/blog/best-bible-app-alternatives
- Olive Tree: major translations and study material are bought one by one (roughly $10 to $20 each in the roundup). A Reddit-aggregator reviewer says most study material has to be bought. https://unstar.app/blog/youversion-bible-gateway-olive-tree-logos-blue-letter-bible-apps-ranked-2026 and https://gummysearch.com/tools/best-products/bible-app/
- Commentary-set price comparison across Accordance, Logos and Olive Tree (price is the only criticism in that review). https://churchtechtoday.com/?p=21752
- Bible Gateway: commentaries, study-Bible notes and audio sit behind the paid Plus tier (weak source). https://unstar.app/blog/youversion-bible-gateway-olive-tree-logos-blue-letter-bible-apps-ranked-2026
- BLB and e-Sword: cost is not the complaint, but licensing leaves out some modern translations (BLB), and e-Sword has fewer books than the paid apps. https://unstar.app/blog/youversion-bible-gateway-olive-tree-logos-blue-letter-bible-apps-ranked-2026 and https://churchtechtoday.com/?p=21752

### 2. Speed, size and bloat (recurring, mostly Logos)

- Logos forum threads call the program slow (a Logos 9 thread, a "loading and functioning extremely slow" thread, and one user reporting about thirty hours of first indexing). Another poster says the installer and library have grown and that many menu tools go unused (excerpt). https://community.logos.com/discussion/comment/1165342 and https://community.logos.com/discussion/comment/1145762
- A Trustpilot reviewer calls it a "monster heavy system". https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/logos.com
- Logos library sync is reported as slow or timing out on libraries of 5,000+ resources (weak source). https://unstar.app/blog/youversion-bible-gateway-olive-tree-logos-blue-letter-bible-apps-ranked-2026
- Contrast: one Reddit-aggregator reviewer likes a simple app because it loads quickly. https://gummysearch.com/tools/best-products/bible-app/

### 3. Sync and data loss (recurring)

- Olive Tree's own help pages warn that reinstalling, or removing and re-downloading a book, can permanently lose annotations that have not synced. They also say sync can fail when notes lack titles. https://help.olivetree.com/hc/en-us/articles/360018619251-Windows-Troubleshooting-and-Support and https://help.olivetree.com/hc/en-us/articles/210470086-Mac-6-14-8-Troubleshooting-and-Support
- Across apps, reviews are said to describe bookmarks, highlights, notes or plan progress missing on a second device for days, and some lost margin notes (weak roundup; its method is unverified). https://unstar.app/blog/youversion-bible-gateway-olive-tree-logos-blue-letter-bible-apps-ranked-2026
- Bible Gateway web-to-mobile sync is reported as slow, sometimes 24 hours or more (weak source, same roundup).
- Olive Tree sync needs an account and re-login, which interrupts reading (same roundup).
- BLB: a review says an account made on the web does not carry to the mobile app. https://www.e-n.org.uk/features/review-blue-letter-bible-versus-logos/
- A search for lost notes in YouVersion found no firm pattern of loss, only the general roundup claim.

### 4. Notes, highlights and format lock-in (recurring)

- Logos forum threads say exported notes and highlights often lack the verse reference, tags or page numbers. Some users call the export tool unusable (duplicates, separate note export). Common workaround: filter, then export to Word or RTF. Users ask for CSV, XML, Markdown, Readwise or Notion export, and one says their notes feel trapped (excerpts). https://community.logos.com/discussion/comment/1167546 , https://community.logos.com/discussion/comment/1176151 , https://community.logos.com/discussion/229001/improve-highlight-export-functionality
- Accordance: a user reports no export function and moving notes by hand (excerpt). https://community.logos.com/discussion/comment/1305503
- theWord imports free e-Sword modules but not modules bought from e-Sword, and has fewer third-party modules. This is module-format friction, not notes. https://puritanboard.com/threads/theword-bible-software.51553/post-665805 and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Word_Bible_Software
- BLB's note and highlight tools are called less polished than Olive Tree's (weak source). https://unstar.app/blog/youversion-bible-gateway-olive-tree-logos-blue-letter-bible-apps-ranked-2026
- e-Sword's notes editor is praised in one review, so this is not a uniform complaint. https://churchtechtoday.com/?p=21752

### 5. Interface, clicks and learning curve (widespread)

- Logos: weeks to months to learn, built for professionals, frequent redesigns, AI features and interface changes disliked by some reviewers. A forum reply says a feature left them lost; another thanks a poster for naming how many steps a workflow takes (excerpts). https://www.teachfloor.com/blog/best-bible-app-alternatives , https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/logos.com , https://community.logos.com/discussion/comment/1308858
- Accordance: steep curve for original-language tools without training; smaller library than Logos. https://www.teachfloor.com/blog/best-bible-app-alternatives
- BLB: dated, information-dense interface that can overwhelm new users. https://www.teachfloor.com/blog/best-bible-app-alternatives and https://www.e-n.org.uk/features/review-blue-letter-bible-versus-logos/
- Olive Tree: dated look and a hard-to-browse resource store (weak roundup). Its original-language parsing tools are called thin by one comparison. https://www.teachfloor.com/blog/best-bible-app-alternatives
- e-Sword: less powerful and less polished than the leading apps. The iPad and iPhone apps have navigation quirks (commentaries and dictionaries cannot be changed from the main Bible window; the Editor tab is awkward to leave). https://churchtechtoday.com/?p=21752
- e-Sword on Mac: older posts show it run through CrossOver with install, fonts, window and search problems. A native Mac version (e-Sword X) now exists. https://www.codeweavers.com/compatibility/crossover/forum/e-sword and https://e-sword.net/mac/faq.htm
- theWord: opinions split between "uncluttered" and "somewhat busy". https://puritanboard.com/threads/theword-bible-software.51553/post-665246

### 6. Distractions, notifications and social features (recurring; mostly YouVersion, ads on Bible Gateway and Bible Hub)

- YouVersion: reviewers reported as objecting that the Friends feed, Prompts, nudges and frequent notifications crowd the reading screen. Streak loss is described as causing anxiety, and plan percentages as rewarding speed (weak roundup). https://unstar.app/blog/youversion-bible-gateway-olive-tree-logos-blue-letter-bible-apps-ranked-2026
- Other write-ups mention social features feeling distracting, and advise turning off all notifications except the Bible app. https://bibleinyear.com/blog/best-daily-devotional-apps and https://www.crosswalk.com/11916677
- Bible Gateway free tier: banner and interstitial ads in chapter view, and Plus upgrade pop-ups mid-reading (weak roundup). Bible Hub: display ads throughout. https://www.teachfloor.com/blog/best-bible-app-alternatives
- Some users prefer the absence of social features (BLB has none). Others, in church settings, name that absence as a gap. https://www.teachfloor.com/blog/best-bible-app-alternatives

### 7. Search, concordance and word-study friction (recurring, thin evidence)

- Logos mobile: a long-press shows a word's Strong's number, but it cannot be tapped to search the resource, which desktop allows. A Strong's number can appear without opening its definition (excerpts). https://community.logos.com/discussion/181403/how-do-i-search-on-a-strongs-greek-number-in-the-lexham-english-bible-in-the-logos-mobile-app
- Counts without lists: a forum user found a lexicon gave an occurrence count but not the verses. Another asked for every reference to a Strong's number in a dictionary (excerpts). https://community.logos.com/discussion/252454/find-all-references-to-a-strongs-number-in-vines-dictionary
- Too many hits: Logos's own guidance says a common-word search can return over a thousand results and recommends narrowing by original-language word. https://www.logos.com/grow/how-to-bible-word-study/
- Chained lookups: in a Strong's app, tapping a number inside a definition makes going back hard (excerpt of an App Store listing). https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1425215177?l=en-us
- BLB: one review says a past update stopped showing where a Strong's number is used in the interlinear for the word being studied (excerpt of an App Store listing). https://apps.apple.com/app/id1673171256
- Accordance: back/forward history clears with each new search; the suggested workaround is the Goto button (excerpt). https://forums.accordancebible.com/topic/26245-fails-to-maintain-place-in-all-books/
- A Reddit commenter picks BLB for word meanings without needing as much familiarity as Logos. https://gummysearch.com/tools/best-products/bible-app/

### 8. Losing your place (recurring across Logos and Accordance)

- Logos: minimizing or switching apps sometimes returns text to an earlier passage; following an unavailable link sends the reader back to the start of the section; read-aloud on Android does not remember location (excerpts). https://community.logos.com/discussion/comment/1242614 , https://community.logos.com/discussion/11930/bug-lost-reading-place-when-following-unavailable-link , https://community.logos.com/discussion/249838/read-aloud-on-android-does-not-remember-location
- Accordance: scroll position resets after sleep; app forgets location when opened to a specific text; text resets to the first verse on reopen (excerpts). https://forums.accordancebible.com/topic/24348-app-forgets-location-when-opened-to-a-specific-text/ , https://forums.accordancebible.com/topic/32823-text-resets-to-first-verse-doesnt-reopen-to-where-i-left/ , https://forums.accordancebible.com/topic/21096-remember-scroll-position
- Church of Jesus Christ Gospel app: opening a link from a page did not preserve position (excerpt). https://tech.churchofjesuschrist.org/forum/viewtopic.php?p=221548
- Workaround users name: keep a bookmark and update it.

### 9. Organizing saved items (scattered; mostly feature requests)

- Logos users ask for folders for bookmarks and favorites, and for notes and reading plans. One built a nested "Favorites" system as a workaround (excerpts). https://community.logos.com/discussion/28948/bookmark-favorites-folders and https://community.logos.com/discussion/252901/mobile-layout-sorting
- A Quran app request says a single bookmark folder is not enough and asks for nested folders. https://feedback.gtaf.org/quran/p/nested-bookmarking-folders
- Apps that offer folders (Life Bible, Bible Memory Verses) are praised for it. https://support.tecarta.com/hc/en-us/articles/4410428701723-Organizing-notes-into-folders
- Bible Hub is said to lack personal highlights, notes and bookmarks. https://www.teachfloor.com/blog/best-bible-app-alternatives

### 10. Platform gaps and mobile as second-class (scattered)

- Logos mobile feels secondary to desktop for study (weak roundup). Bible Gateway's mobile is less polished than a native app. e-Sword's iPad is weaker than Windows, and its mobile apps lack maps. Android has few simple apps (one Reddit-aggregator comment). https://churchtechtoday.com/?p=21752 and https://gummysearch.com/tools/best-products/bible-app/

## Which themes bear on three areas of a new app

These are problems users report, not recommendations about doctrine or content.

### Concordance (English, Hebrew and Greek word lists; every-use pages)

- Counts shown without the verses behind them (theme 7).
- Common words producing result sets too large to read, with narrowing by original-language word as the fix (theme 7).
- Strong's numbers displayed but not tappable, or not opening the definition, especially on mobile (theme 7).
- A chain of lookups with no easy way back (themes 7 and 8).
- Search history that is cleared, or a place lost when a link or search is followed (theme 8).
- Speed on large libraries and first-run indexing (theme 2).
- Exports that drop references (theme 4), which matters if a word list is saved or copied out.

### Word cards (hover or click lookups of Strong's data and verses)

- Number shown but no definition opened; no tap-through on mobile (theme 7).
- Nested lookups with no back path (theme 7).
- Many steps or panels to reach one answer, and users lost after a tool opened (theme 5).
- Screen crowding by pop-ups, nudges or feeds near the text (theme 6).
- Reading place lost after a lookup or a link to unavailable content (theme 8).
- Feature changed or removed in an update (theme 7, BLB interlinear note; theme 5, frequent redesigns).

### Library (saving and organizing pages and saves)

- Notes and highlights failing to sync, or lost on reinstall, with the warnings Olive Tree gives (theme 3).
- Export that loses verse references, tags or page numbers, and wishes for plain formats (theme 4).
- Lock-in worry tied to subscription or account status (themes 1 and 4).
- Flat bookmark lists and requests for nested folders (theme 9).
- Missing features on one platform (theme 10) and slow or timing-out sync at large size (themes 2 and 3).
- Place lost when reopening or returning from a saved item (theme 8).
