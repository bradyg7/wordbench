# Feature designs

Each feature of the app is designed in its own folder here: grilled with the person, prototyped, played, then written
down as *what must be true* ([how work is done](../../docs/agents/how-work-is-done.md#designing-a-feature)). A folder is
written so that a person or an AI can build the feature from it without the conversation that made it.

**No folder is accepted today** (the person, 2026-11-24). Every folder is up for redesign through that process, in the
order set by mapping the whole app ([the turnaround](../../docs/turnaround.md), step 4); the State column below is what each was before. Drawn boards, sheets, review decks
and interaction tables are retired and stay only as history.

Every general feature, button and command is designed here before it is built; [coverage.md](coverage.md) lists
every area of the app and the folder that designs it; [overlaps.md](overlaps.md) lists the parts several folders draw, and
which directions conflict. The Readiness column, [ready-criteria.md](ready-criteria.md), `kit.py`, `lead.py` and the
sheet scripts are retired (2026-11-24) and kept only as history until ticket D removes them.

State is one of: *grilling*, *prototype* (being played), *accepted* (the person's word, dated; ready to build),
*building*, *built* (link the commit), *dropped*. Older folders also say *exploring* or *superseded*.

## Every design meets the real conditions (the person, 2026-11-13)

Something tried alone on an empty desk hides what goes wrong. A prototype, and the *what must be true* items, cover the
feature in each of these:

1. **Among other things open:** beside the reader and the other parts of the app.
2. **Small:** at a small size, in a side part or at phone width.
3. **Years of study:** with a study the size of years of daily use (hundreds of pages, many folders, highlights and
   marks), not a handful of samples. Tests use a generated scratch study of that size, never the person's real folder.

## A design choice is not a setting (the person, 2026-11-13)

A setting is something a person would really want different from someone else (text size, a layout, a key). How the
app is drawn (scroll bars, spacing, the shape of a control) is decided once, in the design, for the whole app, and is
not made a toggle.

## A folder

```
design/features/<feature>/
  README.md     decisions (dated), what the person said at play, what must be true, tickets
  prototype/    the throwaway prototype the person plays
```

Start a new feature by copying [`_template/`](_template/README.md).
