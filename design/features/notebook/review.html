Decisions, 2026-11-20

  • Round 1 answers (2026-11-20).
  • L6: page text and block colors have their own palette; whether it can be the folders' colors (one palette for both) is drawn in round 2 (N9) for the person to choose. The Legend's styles stay for marks.
  • W7: questions become one kind of callout. A callout is a block with a kind; Question and Answered are two of its kinds, beside Key point and Note. The word for this block is open (round 2 offers names; the Library map changes only with the person's go-ahead). > [!question] and > [!done] in today's pages keep reading as before.
  • P6: no covers for now.
  • S2: the side list gets Recents.
  • Build order: the feel and writing first, then the page, then the side list.
  • Round 2 answers (2026-11-20).
  • Callout is the word: /callout makes one; its kinds are Question, Answered, Key point and Note.
  • One palette for annotation. Page and block colors use the same colors as marks and folders, so a color means the same everywhere. A person can give a folder a color, or add one new color to the palette, and the pages in that folder carry it. No free color picker: customizing must not get in the way of organizing or using the app.
  • A toggle is written as <details> in the page (the lead's recommendation): other Markdown tools show it folded.
  • @ for a verse: both forms drawn (12a plain text, 12b a link chip) to compare before choosing.
  • One frame per row. Every row of the table gets its own drawn frame, shown beside the row in a review that moves like a presentation (← →), with a comment box per row. The whole board stays as the overview.

Open questions

  • @ for a verse: 12a or 12b, after seeing both.
Directions

Recommendation

Adopt the feel (F1–F10) and writing (W) lines whole: they are what makes Notion feel like Notion, and round 4 does

not have them. Then round 2 draws them on top of round 4's accepted windows (S28 page, S29 writing, S31 ⋯), so the

interaction table has one row per key, menu item and shortcut. Build order: one menu component (F3), then writing

(W1–W4, W9, W12, W14), then the page (P), then the side list (round 4 S25).

General comment

Arrow keys or the buttons move between slides; 1 to 4 rate the row (Perfect, Satisfactory, Not great, Horrible); N jumps to the next unrated row; G shows every row; Esc leaves a comment box. Comments stay in this browser until you copy or download them.
