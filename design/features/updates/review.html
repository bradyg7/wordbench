# · Decision · Rests on
U-1 · The notice is a dot on the rail's Settings gear, and nothing else outside Settings. Resting on the gear says "Bible Workbench 0.3.0 is ready". Inside the Settings menu, one line at its top says the same and opens the Updates section. No toast, no dialog, no window on start, nothing in the reader, the top bar or the trail line. The system dialog of 248c45a is not built. The rail keeps the gear at every desktop width (a narrow window folds only the place button and the reader's tools: command, C17), so the dot is always on the gear. It sits on the gear's corner; the rail's own small dot under the Trail icon is another thing and the build keeps the two apart. · the constraint (never covers the reader); research theme 6: pop-ups mid-reading and notices crowding the reading screen (research, lines 60-62); Settings is "apart from the study tool" (settings, line 67); the gear on the rail (command, lines 97-98)
U-2 · Updates is one section of the Settings menu, owned by this folder, as Keys is Help's section of the same menu. Settings' own tabs stay as accepted. · overlaps, the Keys row; settings, line 68 (every feature already in Settings stays)
U-3 · The app looks by itself, quietly, and that is a setting. "Look for updates": *When the app opens* (the default) or *Only when I ask*. By itself it looks once, ten seconds after the reader has drawn, and not again within 24 hours. A look that fails (no network, the site down) shows nothing and changes nothing. WB_NO_UPDATE_CHECK=1 turns looking off whatever the setting says. · the person wants the app to update itself; optional per the spec's offline policy; "a design choice is not a setting" (index, lines 59-63): this one a person offline would really want different; 248c45a (ten seconds after the reader, WB_NO_UPDATE_CHECK)
U-4 · A look asks one address for one small file and sends nothing about the person. No account, no licence, no computer id, nothing from the study folder. The section says so in one line. · permanent offline use; research themes 1 and 4 (accounts, lock-in: research, line 121)
U-5 · Nothing is downloaded or installed until the person says Install. There is no "install by itself" setting and no update the app requires. · research: a past update took away what a person used, and frequent redesigns (research, lines 51, 72, 115); the old app keeps working
U-6 · What changed is read from the release, in four fixed headings in this order: Moved or removed, New, Changed, Fixed, then one line, *Your study folder*. Moved or removed is always shown first; when empty it reads "Nothing was moved or removed." The text is plain text written for the release; the window shows it as text only (no links followed, no pictures fetched). Every release newer than the installed one is listed, newest first; older ones are folded to their version and date, and one that moved or removed something still shows that line while folded (one that moved nothing shows only its version and date). The section's card for the newest release shows its version, date and size, its first Moved or removed line (or "Nothing was moved or removed.") and its *Your study folder* line, above Install. The notes speak only of the app. · research (the same lines as U-5): what moved is what people most need to hear; no interpretation (AGENTS.md)
U-7 · Install is two steps, and the person keeps reading through the first. *Install* downloads in the background: Settings can be closed (the section says "You can close Settings and keep reading."), the gear's dot becomes a ring that fills, *Stop* gives it up. When the file is whole and its signature checked, the section offers Restart now and When I close the app. Doing neither is the same as When I close the app. · studying never waits on an update; the open question (now / next start / later) folded to two buttons
U-8 · Before a restart the app saves as it does when a page is closed, and does not restart if a save fails. Every open page's writing is written by the ordinary save; if one cannot be saved the app stays open, names the page ("Phinehas could not be saved, so Bible Workbench was not restarted"), and the update waits ("0.3.0 is still waiting.", with the same two buttons). A line being typed in the command box and a finding being written are kept in the window's storage and are there after the restart. The app reopens as it was: the reader at the same book, chapter and verse, the same panes, tabs and tab in front, floating tabs where they were. Settings is not reopened, and the restart is not a step on the trail. · "never lose the reader's place" (AGENTS.md); the constraint (unsaved writing); research theme 8 (research, lines 78-79); today's flushPageWriters (ui/src/pagewriters.ts:7) and the kept place (ui/src/main.ts:43)
U-9 · After an update nothing opens over the reader. The dot is gone. The Updates section reads "You have 0.3.0, updated today from 0.2.4", with what changed in it, readable offline (the installed version's notes come with the app). · U-1
U-10 · "Not this version" puts the dot away until a newer release. The version stays listed in the section with Install. · the constraint (refused); U-5
U-11 · Every failure is said in the Updates section, in plain words, with what still works. A look by hand with no network: "Could not reach the update site. Bible Workbench works as it is." It is looked for again with Check now. A download that stopped: "The download of 0.3.0 stopped at 31 of 84 MB. Bible Workbench 0.2.4 works as it is.", with Try again. An install that did not finish: the old version opens, and the section says it was not changed. The setting, Check now and U-4's line stay in the section through every failure. The gear's dot shows for a failure only when the person had asked for the install, and goes once the section has been opened (a version still ready keeps its dot); a look made by itself never reports a failure. · the constraint; U-3
U-12 · An update whose signature does not match is thrown away, and there is no "install anyway". The section says: "This update was not installed: it was not signed by Bible Workbench. Nothing was changed." The app also refuses a file that is not newer than what is installed, and an address that is not HTTPS. · the spec's "interrupted or rejected update" (spec, line 73)
U-13 · The version before is kept, once, and the person can go back to it. After an update the section offers "Go back to 0.2.4" (the program files kept beside the app, not downloaded again). It is not offered when the newer version's notes say the study folder's form was moved forward. · research: a removed function has no remedy but the old version (research, line 72); the old app keeps working
U-14 · A copy built from source does not update itself. A build made by scripts/install-app.ps1 has no update code switched on; its section reads "This copy was built from source (40a91dc). It does not update itself." and offers nothing else. · 248c45a: the updater is a Cargo feature, off by default, started only when the build has the key and the address
U-15 · What the app remembers about updates is kept beside the app, not in the study folder: the setting, when it last looked, a version set aside, a downloaded file, the version before (%LOCALAPPDATA%\bible-workbench-rs\updates\). An update writes program files only. If a release ever needs the study folder's form moved, that is its own design with its own go-ahead (AGENTS.md); the updater never does it, and the notes' *Your study folder* line says it before Install. · "nothing in the study folder changes"; AGENTS.md (the study folder's formats)
U-16 · The commands are wb update status, check, notes, install, stop, skip, back, and wb settings updates (below). They work with the window closed. · AGENTS.md: every core action is reachable from wb
U-17 · Where updates come from, as a requirement: one public HTTPS address holding the release file and the installer, GitHub Releases of a public repository that holds releases only; each update signed with one key pair made for updates, whose private half the person holds and which is never in the repository; the public half is compiled into the app and cannot be changed while it runs. The updater offers the same installer the release page offers. · 248c45a's notes (a private repository's files need a token); spec, lines 72-73 (sign the update artifacts; the release page and the updater offer the same approved artifact)
U-18 · An update never asks for activation again, and an activated copy may take every update. · permanent offline use after activation; no renewal or expiry policy was added (spec, line 69). Update entitlement is still listed for the person to confirm in the pilot (spec, line 71)
U-19 · The phone is out of this folder. A phone's updates come from its store. Narrow here means a narrow window on the desktop and the Settings menu at phone width. · coverage (the phone is deferred)

One recommendation, where there was a real choice (U-3, the default): look when the app opens. Reasons: the person

asked for an app that updates itself, and an app that never looks does not; a look costs one small request and never

holds up the reader; it is one switch to turn off. The main risk: a study tool sold as offline makes a network request

nobody asked for. U-4 (it sends nothing) and the line in the section are the answer to it; if the person would rather

the default be *Only when I ask*, only the default changes.
