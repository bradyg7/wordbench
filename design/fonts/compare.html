Reading font

Numbers 25:10–13 (KJV), verbatim from wb text, in each candidate at the phone and desktop reading sizes. Verse 11 carries the green highlight and the Phinehas thread underline, as on the sheets. The chosen font ships inside the app so the iPhone, Android and the desktop look the same. All but Palatino are under the SIL Open Font License.

Merriweather

What the design sheets use. Sturdy, open, made for screens; a little wide.
