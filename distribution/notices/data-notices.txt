BIBLE WORKBENCH — DATA SOURCE NOTICES
Prepared 2026-11-11. Include with the separate software/font/runtime notices.
This file records source attribution and transformations; it is not release approval.

KING JAMES VERSION
King James Version, standardized 1769 text, eBible 2006 Strong's-tagged edition.
Source courtesy of CrossWire Bible Society and eBible.org.
https://ebible.org/eng-kjv2006/copyright.htm
Publisher states public domain outside the UK and identifies UK printing/import restrictions.
Transformation: USFM parsed into indexed verse, word, title and footnote tables;
formatting and Strong's tags are represented as structured fields. Source words
are not replaced with generated interpretations. Exact source hash is recorded
in the accompanying data evidence.

STRONG'S GREEK DICTIONARY IN XML WITH REAL GREEK
Dr. Ulrik Sandborg-Petersen. Version 1.9; source revision dd6758b82f46b620804a8ea677d715844679eea1.
https://github.com/morphgnt/strongs-dictionary-xml
CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Transformation: XML parsed into lemma, transliteration, pronunciation, derivation,
definition and usage fields; cross-references normalized to application tokens.

OPEN SCRIPTURES HEBREW LEXICON
Credit: Open Scriptures Hebrew Bible Project.
HebrewStrong.xml, BrownDriverBriggs.xml, LexicalIndex.xml.
https://github.com/openscriptures/HebrewLexicon/tree/21c9add13bc727d3a951361778e97e3ff7afd1ce
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
The source identifies the underlying BDB and Strong's text as public domain.
Transformation: XML fields imported into the lexicon and BDB tables, linked via
the lexical index; markup and cross-references converted for plain-text display.
Source completion/status labels are retained. TWOT reference numbers are not
reproductions of Theological Wordbook text.

STEP BIBLE
Credit: STEP Bible (https://www.STEPBible.org), based on work at Tyndale House, Cambridge.
TAGNT, TAHOT, TEGMC and TEHMC, source revision 95927044f33c540073246306cea5b13f5a16eb02.
https://github.com/STEPBible/STEPBible-Data
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Changes: tab-separated records imported into SQLite. Greek records are selected
for the Textus Receptus designation, with readable TR meaning/spelling variants
selected where supplied; unsupported variants are omitted. Source-position order
is retained even where TR order differs. Hebrew L/Q/R records are retained;
X/V records are omitted, prefixes are joined, and cantillation is removed.
References follow the source's KJV mapping. Unicode and Strong's identifiers are
normalized. Morphology codes are indexed for lookup. These are application
transformations, not an unchanged mirror of the upstream datasets.

WEBSTER'S UNABRIDGED DICTIONARY
Original text source acknowledgment: Project Gutenberg ebook #29765, credited to
Graham Lawrence, text revision July 6, 2025.
https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/29765
Publisher catalog identifies the original work as public domain in the USA.
Other territories require separate review. This acknowledgment does not imply
sponsorship, endorsement, or distribution by Project Gutenberg.
Changes: independent Bible Workbench parser indexes full original entry blocks;
headword aliases share entries and duplicate senses are retained in source order.
Transport newlines are normalized. Publisher header/footer are excluded from the
definitions. This build does not use the third-party GPL dictionary JSON/parser.
